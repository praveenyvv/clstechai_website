Introduction

Peptides like semaglutide have revolutionized therapies for diabetes and metabolic health, driving massive global demand for scalable, cost-efficient manufacturing. India's pharma sector is a key player in supplying peptide APIs, but manufacturers face a crucial decision: continue with conventional solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS) or adopt advanced microwave-assisted peptide synthesizers.

This analysis compares three industrial peptide synthesis methods targeting 20 kg of crude semaglutide per batch using current 2025 cost data in Indian Rupees (₹ crores). The data demonstrates why the PeptiWave30 30L microwave-assisted peptide synthesizer offers the best balance of cost, throughput, automation, and sustainability.
